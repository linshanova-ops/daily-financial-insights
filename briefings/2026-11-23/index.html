What it implies这意味着

Judgment判断
  • If investors use the lower margin, financed buying can rise into a market that just had a weak Friday. The theoretical 400 billion yuan is not cash that has arrived.若投资者用上更低的保证金，融资买入可能增加，而市场刚刚经历了一个疲弱的周五。理论上的4000亿元还不是已经到来的资金。
  • If the personal-tax jump does not repeat, the October surplus can disappear even if the margin rule stays easier.若个人所得税的跳增不再出现，10月的盈余可能消失，即便保证金规则仍然更松。

Divergences to watch需关注的分歧

Judgment判断

Officials eased financing rules over the weekend after A-shares and Hong Kong shares fell on Friday. The yuan firmed even as the dollar rose on the week. The published goods surplus is smaller than the surplus with gold imports removed.周五A股和港股下跌之后，官员在周末放宽了融资规则。美元全周上涨，人民币却走强。公布的货物顺差小于剔除黄金进口之后的顺差。
