What it implies

Judgment
  • Oil below $90 reduces the immediate inflation impulse, but Hormuz reopening talks and Saudi-smoke reports mean the supply-risk premium is only partly removed.
  • Nvidia's financing web turns AI from a simple growth story into a balance sheet / credit-quality question; equity weakness and CDS widening now tell the same story.
  • The Fed meeting can validate the relief trade only if it treats oil as transitory and capex strength as not inflationary; a hawkish hold would challenge both US multiples and USD/JPY.

Tensions

Judgment

Brent −8.7% settle vs Saudi facility smoke; S&P flat vs Nvidia −5%; Fed hold consensus vs Citadel Securities hawkish-risk warning; Apple defensive mega-cap leadership vs AI circular-financing concern.

What it implies

Judgment
