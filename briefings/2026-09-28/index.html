What moves markets today今日市场驱动

  1. 01

    The official 30-year yield rose to 5.49%

    STRONG强escalated升级USTs

    Fact事实

    • US Treasury：the official 25 September curve printed 4.81% on the 2-year, 4.98% on the 5-year, 5.17% on the 10-year, and 5.49% on the 30-year, after 4.87%, 5.03%, 5.18%, and 5.47% on 24 September.
    US Treasury curve

    Implication含义

    The 10-year eased from 5.18% to 5.17% while the 30-year rose from 5.47% to 5.49%, so the long end made a new high even as the middle of the curve cooled. CICC (24 August): a higher long-end US yield reaches shares through cross-border flows and risk mood, and that link is uneven. USTs carry this steeper official curve. The theme changes if the official 30-year reprints at or below 5.47%.

  2. 02

    Brent settled at 104.32 after Friday's reopening hopes

    STRONG强Oil

    Fact事实

    • EnergyNow：Brent settled at 104.32, down 2.14% from Thursday's 106.60, and WTI settled at 92.41, down 2.33% from 94.61.
    • 华尔街见闻：Trump rejected Iran's plan to reopen the Strait of Hormuz, and Tehran said it would not soften its conditions.
    EnergyNow Friday oil华尔街见闻早餐 9月28日

    Implication含义

    Friday's settle gave back part of Thursday's jump and still closed at 104.32, and the weekend rejection of a Hormuz plan sits beside that lower print. CICC (30 June): the inventory mark from the supply shock can still hold a floor under the price after a reopening. That 104.32 print is the oil exposure. The theme changes if a later Brent settle reprints at or below 100, or back at or above 106.60.

A/H shares and US equities have that package as their trade exposure. The theme changes if the cut is dropped, or if the extension is walked back.

The theme changes if officials stop calling a stronger yen desirable and the 0.6% gain is given back.
