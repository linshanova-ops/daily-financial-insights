Global

Judgment

Regime: The Fed holds at 3.50%–3.75%. June CPI and PPI both missed consensus, and Williams (Jul 15) said inflation likely peaked with policy 'well positioned.' Jul 15 US indexes closed higher while SOX fell 2.08%. Brent settled $84.95 and WTI $79.60 as CENTCOM strikes and the Iran port blockade persisted. Jul 16 brought TSMC's record Q2 ($40.20bn) with an H2 margin warning and BOK's first hike since 2023 (+25bp to 2.75%), sending Kospi −6.37%.

What changed

Fact

What it implies

Judgment
  • TSMC's revenue/capex raise validates AI demand, but H2 margin compression means the market prices profitability, not just top-line — Jul 16 Kospi −6.37% is the cross-asset read-through.
  • BOK tightening into a semi rally creates a regional rate-differential shock for Korea's leveraged chip positioning — especially after Jul 15 CPI relief lifted Kospi +6.24%.
  • Williams' Jul 15 dovish frame supports a Jul Fed hold, but Brent $84.95 settle and BOK hike remind that global inflation risks are not uniformly easing.

Tensions

Judgment
