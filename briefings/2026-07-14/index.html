Global situation

World regime and today's delta

Judgment

Regime: The Fed holds at 3.50%–3.75%. June CPI delivered a broad miss vs consensus — headline 3.5% y/y (vs ~3.8%), core 2.6% y/y (vs ~2.9%) — pulling July hike odds from ~45–50% pre-data to ~16.6% post-data (CME FedWatch via BlockBeats). What changed

Fact
  • June CPI miss across the board: -0.4% m/m headline (vs -0.1% to -0.2% expected), 3.5% y/y (vs ~3.8%); core flat m/m (vs +0.2%), 2.6% y/y (vs ~2.9%); shelter +0.1%; energy -5.7% m/m but +15.7% y/y (BLS; consensus CNBC/Reuters).
  • Post-CPI risk-on close: S&P +0.40% to 7,545.13, Nasdaq +0.92% to 26,110.62, Dow +0.08% to 52,538.58; 10y yield ~4.57% (-~6bp); July Fed hike probability ~16.6% vs ~45–50% Monday (CME FedWatch; Reuters/MarketScreener).
  • Oil premium intact despite fee U-turn: Brent settled $84.73 (+1.72%), WTI $79.34 (+1.54%); Trump dropped the 20% Hormuz cargo fee for Gulf investment deals but kept Iran-only naval blockade (AP/BBC); intraday Brent touched >$86 before settling lower (CBS).
  • Warsh debut + bank earnings: prepared remarks stress restoring price stability; Q&A rejects 'mission accomplished' on CPI; JPM Q2 NI $21.2bn ($7.70 EPS, +41% y/y), Citi $5.8bn ($3.15 EPS, +45% y/y) — both beat on trading/IB strength (SEC filings).

What it implies

Judgment
  • July FOMC hold is now the base case (~83% per CME), but September hike pricing near ~60% (Reuters/FXStreet) means one CPI miss did not kill the tightening narrative.
  • June gasoline-driven relief predates this week's >9% oil spike (Mon) and fresh Hormuz escalation; July CPI will capture forward energy pressure.
  • Strong bank earnings support the 'solid economy' framing in Warsh's MPR, giving the Fed room to stay hawkish rhetorically even if July hike odds collapsed.

Tensions

Judgment

CPI and oil still tell opposite stories — June headline fell on energy that has since surged, and Warsh explicitly warned against declaring victory. Equities closed higher on CPI relief while Brent sits at a one-month high settle; gold rebounded to $4,061 COMEX settle (+1.6%) as yields fell, but the inflation-oil overhang limits the rally's durability.
