Executive summary摘要

The day in five minutes五分钟概览

  • US-Iran fighting reignited over the weekend (Jul 12): Iran claimed the Strait of Hormuz closed; US CENTCOM launched fresh strikes. Brent crude jumped +4% to ~$79/bbl; only 6 vessels transited Hormuz Thu–Fri vs. ~18–22/day earlier in July.

Markets are pricing an inflation shock, not a classic safe-haven event — oil and dollar up, gold lower (COMEX settle near $3,997), copper weak, yields rising.

财经早茶

Global全球

Judgment判断

Regime格局: The Fed is on hold at 3.50%–3.75%, but the market narrative has shifted from "when does easing resume?" to "how soon do hikes return?" Inflation remains the dominant risk — the Fed's July 10 Monetary Policy Report said price increases "stepped up further this spring," driven by tariffs, Middle East energy costs, and AI infrastructure demand. Growth is still expanding, and the labor market is stable (unemployment 4.2% in June), but supply-side inflation shocks are overriding the disinflation story.

What changed有何变化

Fact事实
  • Geopolitical escalation reversed the oil relief trade. After Brent had fallen to ~$70 on Jul 10, weekend hostilities sent it back above $78–79 — roughly 9% above pre-war (late February) levels (Al Jazeera, Reuters).
  • Cross-asset moves confirm inflation repricing, not panic. The dollar index rose to ~101.12, 10-year yields climbed to 4.59%, and COMEX gold settled near $3,997/oz (about -2.6%; early spot prints near $4,070) — consistent with higher real-rate expectations rather than pure risk aversion (Reuters, Dow Jones/Morningstar).
  • Semiconductor euphoria met profit-taking. Friday's US session was strong (S&P +0.42% to 7,575; SK Hynix ADR +13% on Nasdaq debut), but Monday's Kospi plunge (>5%, SK Hynix -10%, Samsung -5–6%) and weak China semis suggest the AI/memory trade is vulnerable to cycle-fear and geopolitical risk (Chosun Biz, Yicai).
  • Fed communication pipeline is loaded. Chair Kevin Warsh testifies before Congress Jul 14 (House, 10am ET) and Jul 15 (Senate) — his first Humphrey-Hawkins hearings, with CPI dropping just 90 minutes before Tuesday's session.

What it implies推论

Judgment判断
  • If Hormuz disruption persists and oil stays elevated, sticky headline inflation could push the Fed toward tightening — the July MPR already flagged that almost all officials would favor some policy firming if inflation stays elevated with a stable labor market.
  • Europe is more exposed than the US to oil shocks; EUR/USD slipped to ~$1.1403 as the euro bloc's energy import dependence bites.
  • Earnings season becomes a higher-stakes test — S&P 500 near highs with Q2 EPS expected +24% y/y; any guidance miss in tech/banks could amplify today's risk-off.

Tensions张力

Judgment判断

Equities and credit are diverging in message — US indices closed at weekly highs Friday while Monday's futures, Asia, and Korea's 18th sell-sidecar of 2026 point to stress beneath the surface. Gold falling alongside oil is the clearest cross-asset warning: this is being read as inflationary, not deflationary.

财经早茶

China中国

Judgment判断

Policy stance政策立场: Accommodative but cautious — liquidity support without rate cuts. The PBOC held the 7-day reverse repo at 1.4% and net-injected CNY217bn Monday after withdrawing >CNY600bn last week. LPR has been frozen at record lows (1-year 3.0%, 5-year 3.5%) for 13 months. The stance says: we'll keep funding conditions loose, but we won't add rate stimulus while Middle East energy shocks feed domestic inflation concerns.

What changed有何变化

Fact事实
  • PBOC flipped from withdrawal to injection — CNY224bn 7-day repos, net +CNY217bn, marking the start of expected tax-period liquidity support (MNI, Xinhua Finance).
  • A-shares opened weak, with a clear sector rotation: Shanghai Composite -0.75% at open; oil/gas, coal, and banks outperformed while semiconductors, AI hardware, and lithium led declines; >3,700 stocks fell (Caixin, Yicai).
  • Liquidity pressure ahead: analysts estimate a >CNY2.2tn funding gap this week from tax payments (Jul 15–17) and government bond issuance (~CNY5,687bn net), suggesting further PBOC operations are likely.
  • External headwind intensified: higher global oil directly pressures China's import bill and PPI; property sector remains a drag.

What it implies推论

Judgment判断
  • China is unlikely to cut LPR at the Jul 22 fixing unless domestic data deteriorates sharply — energy-driven inflation risk argues for patience.
  • Domestic policy may lean sectoral, not broad: managing supply security and sector rotation rather than launching economy-wide stimulus.
  • If the global semis sell-off deepens, A-share tech could underperform H-shares/Kospi recovery — China's onshore market is more exposed to domestic speculative positioning in AI/semis.

Divergences to watch需关注的分歧

Judgment判断

PBOC injecting liquidity while holding rates = "hidden easing" via quantity, not price. A/H spread behavior (Hang Seng only -0.2% vs. deeper mainland tech losses) may reflect foreign vs. domestic sentiment split.
