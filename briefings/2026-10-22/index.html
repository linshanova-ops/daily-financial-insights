Gilts carry the 3.5% print, and long gilts rose on the day the hike case strengthened. The theme changes if CPI is reprinted at or below 3.1%.

    A French 10-year yield through 5.0% is a wider budget premium, and the desk called the France-Germany spread the widest in 15 years. CICC (12 October): the 2027 budget can still pass, but how much fiscal adjustment survives decides how far this premium comes back. OATs carry the 5.0% yield. The theme changes if the French 10-year falls back below 5.0%.

    Raising the floor of the spending guide and creating a non-voting share is equity funding for a build whose cash generation is already tight. CICC (12 October): operating cash flow at the large US AI firms is already near capital spending, so the build draws on both equity and debt. US equities and credit carry the higher floor and the 646.01亿美元 quarter. The theme changes if that floor is cut back below the new guide.
