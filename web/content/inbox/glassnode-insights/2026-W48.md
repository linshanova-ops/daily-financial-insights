Bitcoin is back at its summer lows near $60K, and stocks are now falling too.

This week it gave back the whole NVIDIA rally in less than six hours. The Nasdaq still held most of its gain at that point, but has also lost most of it since.

A convincing close below $60K could open the way to the Realized Price at $53.2K. Every past bear market closed below it.

That is one of eleven analyses in our latest Week On-chain report. Others include:
•  Losses at their heaviest since FTX
•  Most of the long leverage cleared
•  US buyers still missing
