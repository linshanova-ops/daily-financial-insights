Capitulation Meets Support
**************************

Bitcoin crashed to $62K this week, and capitulation is underway.

Realized losses reached a level seen only near past cycle lows. At the low, buyers absorbed the selling in the August block, between $62K and $66K.

That block is now the line to watch. A daily close below it could set off a fast drop toward the Realized Price at $53.7K.
