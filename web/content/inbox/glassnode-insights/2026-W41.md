Recent buyers sold into Bitcoin's breakout.

On Sunday, about 86% of all coins sent to exchanges came from short-term holders in profit, the highest share in a year. If they keep selling at this pace, the next push toward $85K would meet the same supply.

Below price, liquidation levels are stacking up. The nearest cluster sits between about $81.7K and $83.3K, just above the largest bids on the Binance book at $81K, and a slide into it could force leveraged longs to sell.

Those are two pieces of a much bigger picture. The full report covers the whole market this week, from spot volume and new money to options, the order book, macro and altcoins, and how it all fits together.
