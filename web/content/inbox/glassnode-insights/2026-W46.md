Running on Empty
****************

November 11, 2026  •  7 min read  •  Read in browser ( https://research.glassnode.com/the-week-onchain-week-45-2026/?utm_campaign=woc_45_2026&utm_medium=woc_newsletter&utm_source=email )

Bitcoin is down about 15% from its September high, and stablecoin supply is shrinking fast.

Over the past 30 days, total stablecoin supply fell by $14.2B, the fastest contraction since June 2022.

Holders are redeeming stablecoins for dollars and taking that cash out of the market. Should the 30-day change turn back to growth, it would be the first sign that new money is coming in.

That is one of nine analyses in our latest Week On-chain report. Others include:
•  Recent buyers sitting at a loss
•  Two large wallet cohorts starting to buy
•  Where the bids sit below price
