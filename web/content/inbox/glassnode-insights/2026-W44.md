Quiet Under $85K
****************

October 28, 2026 • 9 min read • Read in browser ( https://research.glassnode.com/the-week-onchain-week-43-2026/?utm_campaign=woc_43_2026&utm_medium=woc_newsletter&utm_source=email )

Most long-term Bitcoin holders are underwater.

Long-term holders own about 4M BTC bought between $85K and $118K. More than half of all their coins were bought above today's price.

If price recovers toward $85K, many of these holders may sell to get their money back.

That is one of twelve analyses in the report. Others include:
• Smaller losses with each dip
• Volatility back to summer 2023 levels
• Altcoin breadth fading
