The top buyers still cap Bitcoin near $87K.

Coins bought six to twelve months ago break even near $86.7K, inside a zone of Binance asks and liquidation clusters. A move into that zone could gain speed as shorts are forced to close, but it would run into those asks.

Below price, buyers refilled the $80K bids. The October 15 sweep took out most of that block on the Binance book, just under where the US spot ETFs and treasury companies break even, and within days the bids were back.

Those are two pieces of a much bigger picture. The full report covers the whole market this week, from cost bases, ETF flows and the order book to liquidations, MSCI, macro, tokenized gold, exchange reserves and altcoin leverage, and how it all fits together.
