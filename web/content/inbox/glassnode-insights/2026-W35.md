Squeeze into Supply
*******************

August 26, 2026  •  7 min read  •  Read in browser ( https://x.com/glassnode/status/2092605995356966938?s=20 )

A record short liquidation event ignited a 26% rally off the mid-August low, and real money paid for it: the strongest ETF intake of the year, coins leaving exchanges, every cohort accumulating. What stands between this recovery and the January high is one band of supply between $81K and $86K.

Executive Summary

* A record single-day short flush ignited the rally, which then burned through the liquidation clusters stacked across its path.
* The move was funded: $2.23B of ETF creations over the squeeze window, coins leaving exchanges, and every wallet cohort accumulating at once.
* The cycle composite has climbed out of its cool band, large caps led the rally, and the link to equities collapsed.
* Overhead structure converges: cost-basis shelves, re-laddered asks, the dealer gamma flip and the liquidation shelf all sit between $81K and $86K.
* Options price a hold into late September. The tests: $83.3K above; the $70.0K cost basis and the $62K-65K floor below.
