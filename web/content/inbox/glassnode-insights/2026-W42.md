******************
Q3 2026 Highlights
******************

Hi there,

Q3's headline is Alpha Lab ( https://alphalab.glassnode.com/catalogue?utm_source=email&utm_medium=product_update&utm_campaign=q3_2026_highlights ): an end-to-end research environment on Glassnode data, from metric scan to ranked, backtested trading rules, with or without an AI agent doing the work. Beyond that, we keep expanding our market data and are rolling out orderbook data across spot and perpetual futures. I'd also recommend reading our recent research, in particular The Decentralization Spectrum ( https://research.glassnode.com/ark-invest-glassnode-the-decentralization-spectrum/?utm_source=email&utm_medium=product_update&utm_campaign=q3_2026_highlights ), our report with ARK Invest.

--------
Platform
--------

* Alpha Lab (Beta) Choose an asset, a direction, and the metrics to scan, and get ranked trading rules with return, Sharpe ratio, drawdown, and out-of-sample results. → Read more ( https://research.glassnode.com/glassnode-alpha-lab/?utm_source=email&utm_medium=product_update&utm_campaign=q3_2026_highlights )
* Agentic Payments (x402) Glassnode data is now accessible via x402, a pay-per-call protocol for AI agents. No API key, no subscription, no signup required. Purpose-built for AI agent workflows that need live on-chain and market data on demand. → Read more ( https://research.glassnode.com/agentic-payments-glassnode-data-for-your-ai-agent/?utm_source=email&utm_medium=product_update&utm_campaign=q3_2026_highlights )

-----------
Market Data
-----------

* Perpetual Futures Orderbook Metrics The orderbook suite now extends to perpetual futures, derived from full-depth L2 orderbook data on Binance (BTC and ETH perps). Covers spread ( https://studio.glassnode.com/charts/derivatives.PerpetualOrderbookSpread?a=BTC&utm_source=email&utm_medium=product_update&utm_campaign=q3_2026_highlights ), depth ( https://studio.glassnode.com/charts/derivatives.PerpetualOrderbookDepthQuote?a=BTC&utm_source=email&utm_medium=product_update&utm_campaign=q3_2026_highlights ), imbalance ( https://studio.glassnode.com/charts/derivatives.PerpetualOrderbookDepthImbalance?a=BTC&utm_source=email&utm_medium=product_update&utm_campaign=q3_2026_highlights ) and more, including depth to open interest ( https://studio.glassnode.com/charts/derivatives.PerpetualOrderbookDepthToOpenInterest?a=BTC&utm_source=email&utm_medium=product_update&utm_campaign=q3_2026_highlights ), a metric that flags thin books against large open positions, useful for assessing
liquidation risk.
* Spot & Perpetual Orderbook Heatmaps (Beta) A visual map of resting liquidity across the full orderbook, with bright bands showing where substantial size is concentrated. Lets you watch liquidity build or withdraw ahead of a move in a way a single depth figure can't convey. Available for spot ( https://studio.glassnode.com/charts/market.SpotOrderbookHeatmap?a=BTC&quote_symbol=USDT&resolution=1h&utm_source=email&utm_medium=product_update&utm_campaign=q3_2026_highlights ) and perpetual futures ( https://studio.glassnode.com/charts/derivatives.PerpetualOrderbookHeatmap?a=BTC&resolution=1h&utm_source=email&utm_medium=product_update&utm_campaign=q3_2026_highlights ). Full history back to January 2020 available via Data Shares.
* Expanded Futures Historical Coverage Significant historical backfill across Open Interest ( https://studio.glassnode.com/charts/derivatives.FuturesOpenInterestSum?a=BTC&utm_source=email&utm_medium=product_update&utm_campaign=q3_2026_highlights ), Volume ( https://studio.glassnode.com/charts/derivatives.FuturesVolumeSum?a=BTC&utm_source=email&utm_medium=product_update&utm_campaign=q3_2026_highlights ), Perpetual Funding ( https://studio.glassnode.com/charts/derivatives.FuturesFundingRatePerpetual?a=BTC&utm_source=email&utm_medium=product_update&utm_campaign=q3_2026_highlights ) and more metrics. Largest additions on Gate.com, BitMEX, KuCoin, Deribit, and Kraken.
* Coinbase Premium Index Tracks the relative price gap between BTC on Coinbase and the global multi-exchange price, a reliable signal of US-based buying or selling pressure. → View chart ( https://studio.glassnode.com/charts/market.CoinbasePremium?a=BTC&utm_source=email&utm_medium=product_update&utm_campaign=q3_2026_highlights )
* US Tech 100 Index (Nasdaq) Now available in Studio alongside the S&P 500, VIX, and other TradFi instruments. → View chart ( https://studio.glassnode.com/charts/tradfi.NdxOhlc?utm_source=email&utm_medium=product_update&utm_campaign=q3_2026_highlights )
* New ETF Coverage Morgan Stanley ETFs ( https://studio.glassnode.com/charts/institutions.UsSpotEtfBalancesAll?a=ETH&utm_source=email&utm_medium=product_update&utm_campaign=q3_2026_highlights ) for ETH and SOL added. ETF coverage also now extends to ZEC ( https://studio.glassnode.com/charts/institutions.UsSpotEtfBalancesAll?a=ZEC&utm_source=email&utm_medium=product_update&utm_campaign=q3_2026_highlights ) and TRX ( https://studio.glassnode.com/charts/institutions.UsSpotEtfBalancesAll?a=TRX&utm_source=email&utm_medium=product_update&utm_campaign=q3_2026_highlights ).
* New Treasury Coverage ENA (Ethena) ( https://studio.glassnode.com/charts/treasuries.BalanceCompanies?a=ENA&utm_source=email&utm_medium=product_update&utm_campaign=q3_2026_highlights ) added as a new supported treasury asset, tracking company holdings on-chain.

On-Chain
--------

* Stablecoin & Altcoin Market Cap New aggregated market cap metrics, Stablecoin Market Cap ( https://studio.glassnode.com/charts/market.MarketcapStablecoinsUsd?a=BTC&utm_source=email&utm_medium=product_update&utm_campaign=q3_2026_highlights ) and Altcoin Market Cap ( https://studio.glassnode.com/charts/market.MarketcapAltcoinsUsd?a=BTC&utm_source=email&utm_medium=product_update&utm_campaign=q3_2026_highlights ), for tracking broad market dynamics at a glance.
* Cost Basis Price Series for Bitcoin Two new BTC cost-basis price models: STH Realized Price ( https://studio.glassnode.com/charts/market.PriceRealizedLess155Usd?a=BTC&utm_source=email&utm_medium=product_update&utm_campaign=q3_2026_highlights ), the average on-chain acquisition price of the short-term holder cohort, and True Market Mean Price ( https://studio.glassnode.com/charts/market.PriceTrueMarketMeanUsd?a=BTC&utm_source=email&utm_medium=product_update&utm_campaign=q3_2026_highlights ), also known as the Active-Investor Price.
* Entity-Adjusted LTH/STH URPD Entity-adjusted versions of the URPD for both Long-Term Holders ( https://studio.glassnode.com/charts/indicators.UrpdEntityAdjustedLth?a=BTC&utm_source=email&utm_medium=product_update&utm_campaign=q3_2026_highlights ) and Short-Term Holders ( https://studio.glassnode.com/charts/indicators.UrpdEntityAdjustedSth?a=BTC&utm_source=email&utm_medium=product_update&utm_campaign=q3_2026_highlights ), removing exchange clustering noise for a cleaner view of where coins were acquired.
* Expanded On-Chain Exchange Coverage 15 new exchanges added across ETH, SOL, and DOGE on-chain metrics, including Robinhood (ETH, SOL, DOGE), Bitpanda and bitFlyer (ETH), MEXC (SOL, DOGE), and Coinbase, Kraken, and Gemini (DOGE). → View chart ( https://studio.glassnode.com/charts/distribution.BalanceExchanges?a=DOGE&e=coinbase&utm_source=email&utm_medium=product_update&utm_campaign=q3_2026_highlights )
